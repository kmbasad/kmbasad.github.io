# Brief for the depth pass (poetry plus philosophy)

Read `SKILL.md` first, especially §2a. The essay you are given is already fluent, complete
and lyrical. The author's verdict on this stage: "too lyrical, losing the philosophical
depth." Your job is to make it **think as hard as it sings**.

1. Read the essay and its whole `trellis.md`. Write down for yourself, in one line each, the
   claim of every paragraph. Where a paragraph has no real claim (only images, mood,
   retelling), it must get one or be merged away.
2. Deepen each movement of thought: analysis (the mechanism, the precise distinction),
   diagnosis (what lies underneath, why it recurs), critical thought (the strongest
   objection or counter-case, what must be conceded, where the artist or thinker fails or
   contradicts himself, a judgement), and consequence (what follows). Bring in the trellis's
   own material: its cells and syntheses are the evidence.
3. Keep the voice: every sentence complete, flowing, read-aloud Bangla in the Dhaka
   register; images carry the thinking rather than decorating it; no jargon from the grid;
   zero dashes; colons and semicolons rare; the governing image and the cell links stay
   (adjust links if the sentences around them change; 6 to 12 in all). Length may grow;
   never compress meaning into dense fragments to make room.
4. Do the read-aloud pass on everything you touched, then run the checker to 0 errors:
   `python3 -I .claude/skills/bangla-essay/scripts/check_essay.py <essay.md>`

Report **in English only, no Bangla script**: the essay's central argument in three or four
sentences, the main claims you added or sharpened, the objection(s) the essay now faces, and
anything factual you were unsure of.
