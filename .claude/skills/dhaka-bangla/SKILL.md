# ঢাকার বাংলা: the owner's prose style

The owner (Khan Muhammad Bin Asad) writes Bangla the way educated Dhaka speaks it today. Not
Kolkata's standard চলিত, not the village, and not a mix of the two. This skill is what he
has decided so far. It grows: when he corrects something, change it here (see **Keeping
this skill**), not only in the text in front of you.

Each rule carries where it came from:
- **[owner]**: he said it or wrote it himself.
- **[accepted]**: Claude proposed it on the review page (see Sources) and he left it as it was.
- **[claude]**: Claude chose it while converting a text, following the rules here; he has not
  seen it as a rule yet. Treat it as a default and list it for him when you hand work over.
- **[open]**: not decided yet; ask, or follow the default given and say so.

## The register

- Modern, urban, educated Dhaka speech, written down. Both narration and dialogue are in it;
  dialogue may go further into the spoken language than narration (see "In dialogue").
- **No village forms [owner]:** never কইরা, দিয়া, নিয়া, থিকা, হ্যায়/হেয় (for সে), কইলাম, গেসিলাম.
- **No Kolkata flavour [accepted]:** words such as মশাই, তক্ষুনি, বড্ড are replaced (table below).
- **The -কে stays [owner]:** তাকে, আমাকে, তোমাকে, কাকে. "Modern Dhaka does not say তারে, আমারে."
- **Chandrabindu in words stays [owner]:** পৌঁছা, কাঁদা, হাঁটা, বাঁচা, চাঁদ, পাঁচ. Never drop it.
- **No honorific chandrabindu [owner]:** তার, তাকে, তারা, তাদের, যার, যাকে, যারা, not তাঁর and
  the rest. The same তার serves তুমি, আপনি and তুই alike: speaking to someone as আপনি is no
  reason for তাঁর. "In my Bangla this dot is a little comic." So it may come back now and
  then, as *sarcastic* honour for a pompous bishop or a self-important philosopher, and
  only rarely. ওঁর, ওঁকে, এঁর become উনার, উনাকে [accepted];
  উনি and ইনি stay.
- **সঙ্গে and সাথে are both fine [owner].** Do not change one into the other for its own sake.
- **Words today's Dhaka knows [owner]:** old or bookish words go even when they are correct.
  His example was সহিস: "unknown; I want a more familiar word; even আস্তাবল is no longer
  familiar". Say it plainly instead (যারা ঘোড়া দেখাশোনা করত) and keep the meaning. Names,
  titles and an author's own terms of art stay as they are.
- **Bangladeshi spelling where it differs [owner]:** উঠান, not উঠোন. Further cases are [open];
  don't generalise this to পুরোনো and the like on your own.
- **No English code-mixing** in literary prose. He mixes in chat ("সো", "ইউজ"); prose does not.

## Verbs: the heart of it

Dhaka says **করছি** for standard করেছি. So the standard progressive করছি cannot stay: it
would be read as a perfect. Everything else follows from that.

| | standard | Dhaka | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| progressive, consonant root | করছি, করছে, করছেন | **করতেছি, করতেছে, করতেছেন** | [owner] |
| progressive, তুমি | করছ | **করতেছ** | [owner] |
| progressive, vowel root | খাচ্ছি, যাচ্ছে, দিচ্ছে, হচ্ছে | unchanged (খাইতেছি, যাইতেছি also fine) | [owner] |
| past progressive | করছিল, বলছিলেন | **করতেছিল, বলতেছিলেন** | [accepted] |
| perfect | করেছি, করেছে, দেখেছেন | **করছি, করছে, দেখছেন** | [accepted] |
| perfect, তুমি | করেছ | **করছ** | [owner] |
| past perfect | করেছিল, ভেবেছিলাম | **করছিল, ভাবছিলাম** | [accepted] |
| perfect of vowel roots | হয়েছে, দিয়েছে, নিয়েছে, খেয়েছে, পেয়েছে, চেয়েছে | **হইছে, দিছে, নিছে, খাইছে, পাইছে, চাইছে** | [accepted] |
| perfect of যাওয়া | গিয়েছে, গিয়েছিল | **গেছে, গেছিল** | [accepted] |
| perfect of আসা, আনা | এসেছে, এনেছে | **আসছে, আনছে** | [accepted] |
| perfect of -আনো verbs | দেখিয়েছে, হারিয়েছিল, শুনিয়েছি | **দেখাইছে, হারাইছিল, শুনাইছি** | [accepted] |
| simple past | করল, বলল, হলো | **করল, বলল, হল** (no ো) | [owner] |
| simple past, তুমি | করলে | **করলা** (when তুমি is the subject) | [accepted] |
| past of আসা | এল, এলেন, এলাম | **আসল, আসলেন, আসলাম** | [accepted] |
| past of খাওয়া, পাওয়া | খেল, পেল, খেলাম | **খেল, পেল, খেলাম** as standard; খাইল/পাইল only for a reason (below) | [owner] |
| habitual past | করত, হতো | **করত, হত** (no ো) | [owner] |
| habitual past, তুমি | করতে | **করতা** (when তুমি is the subject) | [accepted] |
| habitual of যাওয়া, খাওয়া, পাওয়া | যেত, খেত, পেত | **যেত, খেত, পেত**; যাইত now and then, where he wants it more spoken | [owner]: "বেশির ভাগ সময় আমি যেতই বলি, মাঝে মাঝে যাইত যেখানে একটু বেশি কলোকুয়াল হতে ইচ্ছা করবে" |
| future, আমি | করব, বলব | **করব, বলব** (no ো) | [owner] |
| future of দেওয়া, নেওয়া | দেব, দেবে, নেব | **দিব, দিবে, নিব** | [owner] |
| future, তুমি | করবে | **করবা** (when তুমি is the subject) | [accepted] |
| আপনি's imperative | করুন, দিন, বসুন, শুনুন | **করেন, দেন, বসেন, শোনেন** | [accepted] |
| negative past | করেনি, হয়নি, দেখিনি | **করে নাই, হয় নাই, দেখি নাই** | [accepted]; joined or apart is [open], he wrote "দাওনাই" in chat |
| নেই | নেই | **নাই** | [accepted] |
| রয়েছে meaning "is", "there is" | রয়েছে | **আছে** (রইছে only for "stayed") | [claude] |
| a blessing or wish | খোদা … করুন | **করুক**, not আপনি's করেন | [claude] |
| infinitive, conditional | করতে, হতে, যেতে, খেতে, পেলে | unchanged: **যেতে, খেতে, পেতে, পেলে** (not যাইতে, খাইতে) | [owner], following his যেত/পেল/খেল; হতে, not হইতে |
| verbal noun | দেওয়া, নেওয়া | **দেয়া, নেয়া** | [accepted] |
| first person present of দেওয়া, নেওয়া | দিই, নিই | **দেই, নেই** | [accepted] |
| তুমি present | আছ, করো | **আছ, করো** (no ো added to আছ) | [owner] |

- **The owner's spelling [owner]:** no ো-কার added to an ending: করল, করত, করব, করছ, আছ.
- **The ই-forms are seasoning, not the dish [owner]:** যাইত, খাইল, পাইল and খাইতে are Dhaka, but he
  uses them only now and then, for a more spoken touch. Write the standard যেত, খেল, পেল and
  খেতে by default. The perfects খাইছে, পাইছে and দিছে stay, since they are the core Dhaka perfect.
- **Sound decides too [owner]:** he kept "দেখতে পাইল" right before "পাংলস" for the echo
  (পাইল/পাংলস, and "piles" under it). Where a form makes a pun or an echo, keep it, even
  against the default, and don't flatten it on a later pass.
- **তুই [accepted, use open]:** the paradigm is in `forms.tsv`: করিস, করতেছিস, করছিস, করলি, করতি,
  করবি, কর, করিস নাই; আয়, যা, দে, নে. Where তুই belongs (master to servant, insults, close
  friends) is [open]. Follow the source text's own intimacy, and when unsure keep তুমি.
- **The new progressive and perfect can clash in speech [claude]:**
  - মানতেছি (j'avoue) reads oddly; মানলাম is how Dhaka concedes a point.
  - দেখতেছ তো / দেখতেছেন তো become দেখলা তো / দেখলেন তো.
  - Dhaka has no চলছি for "am going", since চলছি reads as a perfect: say যাচ্ছি.
  - Where a progressive was used only for colour (সবসময়ই থাকতেছে), the plain present (সবসময়ই আছে) is better.
- **Both may be right [owner]:** where Dhaka has two living forms (খাচ্ছি / খাইতেছি,
  হতে / হইতে), either is fine. Prefer the lighter one in narration. Don't make the text
  uniform by force.

The full table, every form of the 207 verbs found in Candide and the model paradigms,
is `forms.tsv`: standard form, Dhaka form, verb, category, status, and how often it
occurs in Candide. A standard form with several rows has several readings, and the
sentence decides between them:
- **-তে:** an infinitive (করতে, unchanged), or তুমি's habitual past (করতা).
- **-লে:** a conditional (করলে, unchanged), or তুমি's past (করলা).
- **-বে:** সে's future (করবে, unchanged), or তুমি's future (করবা).

## Other words

`words.tsv` lists them with their status. In short [accepted]:
- **Kolkata words:** মশাই → সাহেব, মশাইরা → সাহেবরা; তক্ষুনি → তখনই, এক্ষুনি → এখনই; অমনি → সাথে সাথে;
  মোটে → মাত্র; বড্ড, ভীষণ → খুব; ঢের → অনেক; খানিকটা → কিছুটা.
- **Food and invitations:** নিমন্ত্রণ → দাওয়াত; জলখাবার → নাস্তা; জল → পানি.
- **People:** গিন্নি → বউ; ঝি → কাজের মেয়ে.
- **Only when the sense fits:** ভারি → খুব (only when it means "very"; ভারী = heavy stays);
  বটে → ঠিকই; বৈকি → অবশ্যই; দিব্যি → বেশ; বাছা → বাবা (as a form of address).
- **-গুলো, -গুলা [owner]:** "I never say গুলো, I say গুলা; গুলা doesn't sound good either, so
  mostly I avoid the whole thing." His own fix: দুর্গগুলোর মধ্যে সবচেয়ে সুন্দর → **সব দুর্গের মধ্যে**
  সবচেয়ে সুন্দর. Reword: use সব + noun; লোকেরা/লোকজন for people; এসব/ওসব/সেসব/যেসব for
  এগুলো/সেগুলো; or a bare noun where the plural is clear. Where a marker is really needed,
  write **-গুলা**, "মাঝে মাঝে… তবে অনেক বেশি না". Never write -গুলো.
- **-টি → -টা:** একটি → একটা, দুটি → দুইটা, তিনটি → তিনটা, প্রতিটি → প্রতিটা, লোকটি → লোকটা. But রুটি, মাটি and
  খাঁটি are words, not -টি.
- **Left as they are:** কোথায়, এখানে, সেখানে, হ্যাঁ, হুজুর, খোদা.
- **Titles and names [owner]:**
  - **ব্যারনেস** is the main word; ব্যারনপত্নী only in mockery, as Pangloss's "সম্ভাব্য সব
    ব্যারনপত্নীর মধ্যে সেরা ব্যারনপত্নী".
  - **কুনেগোঁদ**, not কুনেগোন্দ ("সব জায়গায়"). The rule behind it, that French nasal
    vowels take a চন্দ্রবিন্দু as in কাঁদিদ and মার্তাঁ, is [claude]. পাংলস stays as it is, since
    he hasn't asked for পাঁগ্লস.
  - **অধিধর্মবিশ্বগাধাতত্ত্ব** is his own word for métaphysico-théologo-cosmolonigologie. It shows the
    principle for an author's mock-learned coinage: keep it pompous, one compound, with the
    plain insult (গাধা) hidden inside, where the original hides it.
- **পিছন, not পেছন [owner].**
- **The Kolkata -এ ending gives way to the Bangladeshi -আ:**
  - **খিদা, not খিদে [owner]:** "খিদা বা ক্ষুধা দুটাই হবে, তবে খিদে না". ক্ষুধা is fine where the register is higher.
  - **The same pair elsewhere [claude]:** ইচ্ছা, মিথ্যা, দিশাহারা, ফিতা.
  - **The -ও ending is [open]:** পুরোনো/পুরানো, টুকরো/টুকরা, মুক্তো/মুক্তা, কৌটো/কৌটা, ধুলো/ধুলা.
- **Say whose [owner]:** he made "কাছে একটা পয়সাও নাই" into "**তার** কাছে একটা পয়সাও নাই". Where
  the English or French drops a possessor or subject that Bangla speech would say, put it in.
- **Old foreign coins: say what they are [owner]:** écu became রুপার মুদ্রা, not এক্যু (and
  দশটা পাতাগোন এক্যু became দশটা রুপার মুদ্রা). Whether পিস্তোল, পিয়াস্তার and ফ্লোরিন go the same way is [open].
- **Words coined by an earlier draft may stay [owner]:** he kept আড়ালপর্দা for paravent when
  offered ভাঁজ-করা পর্দা. Don't replace a working coinage only because it is new.
- **Added while converting Candide [claude]** (`words.tsv` lists them with status `claude`):
  - **Numbers:** দুটো → দুইটা, তিনটে → তিনটা.
  - **Kolkata words:** ভাইপো → ভাতিজা, মাইনে → বেতন, পণ্ডিতমশাই → মাস্টার সাহেব, পাঠশালা → স্কুল, ছুতোর → কাঠমিস্ত্রি.
  - **Bangladeshi spellings:** ইচ্ছা, নাস্তা, ভিখারি, পূজা, চৌদ্দ, জুতা, খুচরা, তাড়াহুড়া, ন্যাংটা.
  - **Old words:** সওদাগর → ব্যবসায়ী.
- **হোটেল [owner]:** for an inn, a tavern or an eating-house (cabaret, auberge, hôtellerie) alike,
  since in Bangladesh হোটেল is both where you eat (ভাতের হোটেল) and where you stay. Not
  সরাইখানা or শুঁড়িখানা. Use মদের দোকান only where the drinking is the point.

  Not generalised: পুরোনো, টুকরো, কৌটো and মুক্তো stay until he says otherwise.
- **Homographs [claude]:** these look like verb forms but often aren't. The lint and
  `convert.py` leave them to you:
  - দিন: "day", far more often than "please give".
  - দেবে: "sinks" (কাদায় দেবে গেল), not only "will give".
  - পেতে: in ওত পেতে it comes from পাতা.
  - চেয়ে: "than".
  - পরে: "after".
  - মানে: "meaning".
  - বাড়ি: "house".
  - ভেড়া: "sheep".
  - গলা: "throat".

## In dialogue

Dialogue may sound more spoken than narration. Where `words.tsv` gives a dialogue form,
use it inside speech. Otherwise:
- **Particles:** place তো, না?, নাকি, তাই না?, আরে and আচ্ছা only where a Dhaka speaker
  would say them.
- **Additions:** don't add speech that isn't in the source.
- **Finding the speech:** the text rarely marks dialogue (Candide follows the French, with
  an em-dash at most). Tell it by reading.

## Converting a standard text

1. **First pass, by script:** `python3 -I .claude/skills/dhaka-bangla/convert.py FILE`
   (`--col N` for a TSV). It changes only what is certain: forms whose every reading has
   one Dhaka form, honorifics, and the safe words. It matches whole words only.
2. **Read every sentence**, the original beside the first pass, and decide what needs the
   sentence:
   - তুমি forms (-তে, -লে, -বে)
   - words that depend on their sense (ভারি, বটে)
   - dialogue forms
   - the rare sarcastic তাঁ
   - anything the script got wrong
3. **Keep the content.** One sentence stays one sentence, with the same meaning, the same
   names and the same marks (`_italics_`, `*` footnotes). This is a change of voice, not a
   new translation.
4. **Read it back as Dhaka speech.** If a sentence now sounds forced, or half Kolkata and
   half Dhaka, smooth it. But stay with the forms above rather than inventing new ones.
5. **Lint:** `python3 -I .claude/skills/dhaka-bangla/lint.py FILE` (`--col N` for a TSV) lists
   standard forms that are still there. "replace" is certain. "check" depends on the
   sentence, so look at each. A Dhaka form spelt like a standard one (করছিল) is never
   flagged, because only the source text can show which was meant. Compare with the
   original for those.

## Keeping this skill

- **When the owner corrects a form or a word,** in a review, a comment or a chat:
  - change the rule above, and mark it [owner];
  - change its rows in `forms.tsv` or `words.tsv`, setting status `owner`;
  - add a line to the log below.

  One correction often stands for a pattern ("আমারে না, আমাকে" ruled out every -রে), so
  apply it to the whole pattern and say that you did.
- **When his own writing shows a form** (in chat or in his edits), you may note it under
  "Seen in his writing", but don't turn it into a rule until he confirms it.
- **Don't widen a rule on your own.** Add [open] questions instead.
